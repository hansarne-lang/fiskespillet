Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: A single visual benchmark reference image for FISKELAGET, a realistic colorful Caribbean multiplayer fishing game. This image establishes the color, lighting, boat design and material standard for future game assets.
Primary request: Create a beautiful, richly colorful, physically believable high-end real-time 3D game environment render. One coherent landscape image, approximately 16:9, not a collage or design sheet.
Scene/backdrop: A compact fictional Caribbean marina on a small tropical island. Crystal-clear shallow turquoise water with visible sand and a few submerged rocks becomes emerald, then deep cobalt blue toward the open sea. Fine pale warm sand, lush green palms and a few small wooden waterfront boathouses painted coral, turquoise and warm yellow. Open horizon and a bright blue sky with soft small clouds.
Subject: A handsome realistic approximately 26-foot starter sport-fishing boat moored alongside a wooden dock. The whole boat is clearly readable in a three-quarter view from dock eye level. Ivory fiberglass hull with a tasteful turquoise side accent, modest practical canopy or T-top, believable center console, outboard engine, rubber fenders, sensible cleats and mooring rope, two correctly mounted fishing rods with reels and fine lines. Human-scale, attainable and practical, not a superyacht. One naturally posed adult angler in a pale coral fishing shirt and cream hat aboard gives scale, with believable hands and anatomy.
Composition/framing: A fixed gameplay-like dockside viewpoint, natural 35mm perspective, medium-wide. The boat is the main subject and occupies enough of the frame to judge its materials and construction. Weathered warm timber dock in the foreground, colorful boathouses and foliage in the middle distance, inviting sea beyond. All elements sharp enough for asset evaluation; unobstructed silhouette; natural visual hierarchy; clean horizon.
Lighting/mood: Warm clear early-morning sunshine, luminous soft shadows that retain color and detail, restrained sparkling reflections, visible water transparency, natural ambient light. Peaceful, welcoming, joyful fishing holiday, polished and beautiful.
Color palette: Shallow turquoise #27C9C2, emerald #168D82, deep ocean #1769A5, warm sand #F3D7A2, lush foliage #398552, coral paint #E97963, sunshine yellow #F1C65D, ivory #F6F0DF. Palette swatches are guidance, do not draw them. Rich balanced saturation, strongest color in water, vegetation and painted details; neutral ivory boat surfaces and warm timber balance it.
Materials/textures: Physically based realistic fiberglass with subtle roughness and gentle highlights, faint salt traces near the waterline, fine wood grain and mild sun wear, brushed stainless steel, textured rope fibers, natural fabric weave, soft leaf translucency. Consistent scale and texel detail. High craft, not sterile catalog plastic.
Constraints: Natural real-world proportions, plausible construction and marine equipment, coherent realistic 3D aesthetic, game asset quality benchmark. The water, light and colorful environment must be as beautiful as the boat. No HUD, no lettering, no labels, no logo, no watermark, no split panels, no swatch strips. Avoid cartoon shapes, exaggerated toy geometry, neon clipping, excessive bloom, crushed shadows, heavy fog, exaggerated depth of field, orange cinematic color grading, extravagant fantasy architecture, impossible rigging, duplicate engines or malformed rods.
